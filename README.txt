1.  WHAT YOU RECEIVED
--------------------------------------------------------------
  GameCafeManager.exe     - the complete program. Nothing else
                            needs to be installed.
  Your PRODUCT KEY        - sent to you separately by your provider.

Copy GameCafeManager.exe to your computer (for example the
Desktop) and double-click it to start.


--------------------------------------------------------------
2.  FIRST START - ACTIVATION (one time only)
--------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Double-click GameCafeManager.exe.
  2. The "Activate your copy" window appears.
  3. Copy the whole product key you were sent and paste it into
     the box (right-click > Paste, or press Ctrl+V).
  4. Click ACTIVATE.

The program now opens, and it will not ask for the key again on
this computer.

Tips
  - Paste the COMPLETE key. It is long (about 130 letters in
    groups of 5). Do not retype it - copy and paste.
  - Spaces, dashes and new lines do not matter.

3.  DAILY USE
--------------------------------------------------------------
The program has four tabs at the top:
  Stations  |  Food & Drinks  |  Daily Report  |  Settings

STATIONS  (the main screen)
  Every pool table, PS5, PC and car simulator is shown as a card.
  - FREE stations can be started. OCCUPIED stations show a live
    timer. PAUSED stations show in gold.
  - To START a session: click a free station, enter the customer
    name (phone is optional), choose what they are playing, check
    the hourly rate, and click "Start session".
  - Inside a running session you can:
      * Pause / Resume the timer (paused time is NOT charged)
      * Add food & drinks to the order
      * Change the game being played
      * Cancel the session (no charge)
      * End the session and bill
  - To END a session: click "End session & bill". You will see
    the time charge, the food & drinks total, and a Discount box.
    Type the discount as a PERCENTAGE (for example 10 for 10% off).
    Choose how the customer paid (Cash / UPI / Card), then confirm.
    A receipt is shown, and a copy is saved on your computer
    (see section 5).

FOOD & DRINKS
  - Tabs for Snacks, Drinks and Smokes list your items and prices.
  - To add an item to a customer's order: choose the station in
    "Order for", then double-click the item (or select it and
    click "Add to order").
  - "New item", "Edit" and "Delete" let you change your menu.

DAILY REPORT
  - Shows sales for one day: totals, and every session.
  - Use the < and > buttons (or the left/right arrow keys) to move
    between days, and "Today" to come back.
  - "Export CSV" saves the day's sales as a file you can open in
    Excel.

SETTINGS
  - Currency symbol.
  - Time billing: charge exact minutes, or round up to 5 / 15 /
    30 minutes or 1 hour.
  - "Business day starts at": sessions that end before this hour
    count towards the previous day (useful if you close after
    midnight).
  - Stations: add, edit or delete tables, consoles, PCs and
    simulators and set their hourly rate. A changed rate only
    affects sessions started afterwards.
  Click "Save settings" after changing the billing options.


--------------------------------------------------------------
4.  KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS
--------------------------------------------------------------
Press F1 at any time to see the full list. The most useful:

  Station number + Enter   Open that station
  Station number + P       Pause / resume that station
  Ctrl + 1 / 2 / 3 / 4     Stations / Food / Report / Settings
  F2                       Pause / resume (inside a session)
  F4                       End session and bill
  F11                      Full screen on / off
  Esc                      Leave full screen / go back

The program starts in full screen. Press F11 or Esc to get a
normal window.


--------------------------------------------------------------
5.  YOUR DATA AND BACKUPS
--------------------------------------------------------------
All your data (stations, menu, sales) is stored on this computer in:

      C:\Users\<your Windows name>\GameCafeManager\

  cafe.db      - your stations, menu and all sales records
  receipts\    - a text copy of every receipt

To open the folder quickly: press Windows key + R, type
      %USERPROFILE%\GameCafeManager
and press Enter.

BACK UP regularly (at least once a week):
  Copy the whole GameCafeManager folder to a pen drive or cloud
  storage. Close the program first for the safest copy.

MOVING TO A NEW COMPUTER:
  1. Copy GameCafeManager.exe to the new computer.
  2. Copy your saved GameCafeManager folder to the same place on
     the new computer (C:\Users\<name>\).
  3. Start the program. If it asks for activation, contact your
     provider for a key for the new computer.

Do not delete this folder - it contains your sales history.


--------------------------------------------------------------
6.  YOUR LICENSE
--------------------------------------------------------------
  - Your license is tied to the product key you were given.
  - The program checks your license when it starts and every few
    minutes while running. This needs an internet connection.
  - If the internet is down, the program keeps working. It only
    asks you to connect to the internet if it has not been able
    to verify the license for about 30 days.
  - SUBSCRIPTION licenses show a warning during the last 14 days
    before they expire. Contact your provider for a renewal key
    before the date.
  - LIFETIME licenses never expire.
  - Do not share your product key. If a key is misused, it can be
    deactivated, and the program will stop working.

If the program shows "This product key has been deactivated" or
"This license expired", contact your provider (details at the top
of this guide).

7.  TROUBLESHOOTING
--------------------------------------------------------------
"Windows protected your PC" appears when I start the program
  This is normal for new programs. Click "More info", then
  "Run anyway".

My antivirus blocked or removed the file
  Restore it from the antivirus quarantine and add
  GameCafeManager.exe to the antivirus "allowed" list, or ask your
  provider for help.

"That product key is not valid"
  The key was cut short or mistyped. Copy the complete key again
  and paste it. Do not retype it.

"This product key was issued for a different computer"
  Your key was made for another computer. Contact your provider.

"Please connect this computer to the internet to verify your
license"
  Connect to the internet and start the program again.

The program fills the whole screen and I want a window
  Press F11 (or Esc).

A rate or setting did not change
  Click "Save settings". Station rate changes apply to new
  sessions only.

The program closed with a license message while I was working
  Your data is saved. Restart the program. If it says the key is
  deactivated or expired, contact your provider.
